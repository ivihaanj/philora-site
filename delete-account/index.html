Delete your Philora account and data

App: Philora

Developer: Vihaan Joshi

You can delete your Philora account and the data linked to it at any time. You do not need the app installed to ask.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Philora and sign in.
  2. Go to You → Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Tap Delete account and data.
  4. Confirm. Your account and its data are deleted straight away.

Option 2: Without the app

Send an email to philora.app.team@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Philora account", from the email address you signed up with (or the Gmail address you used with "Continue with Google"). We delete your account within 7 days and reply to confirm. We may ask you to confirm from that email address so that nobody else can delete your account.

If you used Philora without signing in, we never received your data: it lives only on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

What we delete

  • Your account (account ID, email address, password hash, Google sign-in link)
  • Your display name
  • Your synced learning progress: lessons, quiz, trial and game results, streaks, badges, coins, daily goal
  • Your device records

What we keep, and for how long

  • Security and sign-in logs (IP address, time, sign-in events): up to 1 year, then deleted.
  • Backups: deleted data disappears from our hosting provider's backups within 30 days.
  • A deletion record containing no personal data (a one-way hash of the account ID and the date), to prove the deletion happened.
  • Emails you sent us (including this request): up to 2 years.
  • Nothing is kept for advertising. We do not sell data.

Delete some data without deleting your account

Email philora.app.team@gmail.com and tell us what to delete. Your answers to "pick a side" questions never leave your phone, so there is nothing of them on our servers to delete.
