Philora Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Who we are

Philora is a philosophy-learning app made by Vihaan Joshi, an individual developer based in India ("we", "us"), and developed together with Shaurya Sultania. We decide how your data is used. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 we are the "Data Fiduciary", and under the EU and UK GDPR we are the "controller".

  • Privacy questions and requests: philora.app.team@gmail.com
  • Grievance Officer (India): Vihaan Joshi, philora.app.team@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • You can use Philora without an account. Your progress is then stored only on your phone.
  • If you create an account, we store your learning progress on our servers so you can keep it on any device.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not track you across other apps or websites. There are no analytics or advertising tools in the app.
  • Your answers to "pick a side" and "what do you think" questions always stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app (You → Settings) or by email.

4. Children and teens

Philora is meant for people 13 and older. - Before creating an account you must confirm that you are 18 or older, or that your parent or guardian has agreed to you making an account. - Without an account, the app works fully on your phone and we receive no personal data. - We never use anyone's data, including teens' data, for advertising, profiling or behavioural tracking.

If you are a parent and believe your child gave us data without your permission, write to philora.app.team@gmail.com and we will delete it.

6. International transfers

Your account data is stored in India (Mumbai). Some providers (Brevo for emails, Google if you use "Continue with Google") may process data outside your country. For transfers out of the EU or UK we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum) in our providers' data processing terms. Transfers from India follow section 16 of the DPDP Act.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to: - access your data and get a copy (email us); - correct it (edit your display name in the app, or email us); - delete it (You → Settings → Delete account and data, or see our account deletion page); - withdraw consent (turn off reminders at any time; this does not affect earlier processing); - object to or restrict processing based on legitimate interests; - data portability (we provide your data in JSON, a common machine-readable format); - nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or cannot act (India); - complain to a regulator: in India, the Data Protection Board of India; in the EU, your local data protection authority; in the UK, the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Email philora.app.team@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We reply within one month (GDPR) and in any case within 90 days (India). We may ask you to confirm the request from your account email.

California residents: you have the rights to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Each user can only read their own records (database row-level security), and requests are rate-limited to prevent abuse. Only the developer can access production data. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you without undue delay by email, explain what happened and what you can do, and notify regulators as the law requires (in India, the Data Protection Board within 72 hours).

10. Notifications

Reminders are optional. The app asks for permission only when you switch the reminder on. You can turn it off in the app or in your phone's settings.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes (for example, adding analytics, crash reporting or subscriptions), we will update this page and tell you in the app before the change applies, and ask for consent where needed.
